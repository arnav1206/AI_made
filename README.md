# 🎙️ FormMitra – AI-Powered Multilingual Voice Form Filling Assistant

> **Making digital forms accessible to every Indian through multilingual conversational AI.**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/🚀%20Live%20Demo-FormMitra-success?style=for-the-badge)](https://formitra.streamlit.app)

## 🌐 Live Demo

🔗 **Try FormMitra here:** https://formitra.streamlit.app

FormMitra is an AI-powered multilingual voice assistant that enables users to complete digital forms simply by speaking in their preferred Indian language. Instead of navigating complex interfaces and typing information manually, users can have a natural conversation with an intelligent AI assistant that understands context, extracts relevant details, asks follow-up questions when necessary, and automatically fills application forms.

Powered by **Google Gemma**, FormMitra bridges the gap between technology and citizens by making government, banking, healthcare, education, and enterprise services accessible to everyone—regardless of language proficiency or digital literacy.

---

## 🌟 Problem Statement

Millions of Indians face challenges while filling online and offline application forms due to:

- 🌐 Language barriers
- 💻 Limited digital literacy
- ⌨️ Complex English-only interfaces
- 📝 Lengthy and confusing forms

Whether applying for government schemes, banking services, healthcare registrations, scholarships, or official documents, users often struggle with traditional digital forms.

**FormMitra solves this problem by allowing users to fill forms using only their voice in their preferred Indian language.**

# 🧠 Why Google Gemma?

Google **Gemma** serves as the intelligent reasoning engine behind FormMitra.

Unlike traditional speech recognition systems, Gemma:

- Understands multilingual conversations
- Maintains conversational context
- Extracts structured information from natural speech
- Identifies missing details
- Asks relevant follow-up questions
- Validates collected information before form submission

This makes the interaction feel like talking to a real assistant instead of filling a rigid digital form.

# 📌 Workflow

1. User selects their preferred language.
2. User speaks naturally.
3. Speech is converted into text.
4. Gemma extracts required information.
5. Missing information is identified.
6. AI asks intelligent follow-up questions.
7. Information is automatically mapped to the form.
8. Completed details are read back for verification.
9. A verified PDF is generated for download or submission.
